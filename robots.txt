# reviewsforsale.com
#
# This domain is operated as an awareness initiative of the Coalition for
# Trusted Reviews (coalitionfortrustedreviews.com), whose members include
# Amazon, Booking.com, Expedia Group, Glassdoor, Tripadvisor and Trustpilot.
#
# The pages at the root of this domain present a simulated review-selling
# storefront. No reviews are sold and no payments are accepted; any attempt
# to place an order returns a warning explaining that purchasing fake
# reviews is unlawful. The /answers/ section publishes the sourced
# educational material behind that warning.
#
# Identical content is served to crawlers and to human visitors.
